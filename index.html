01 / Mission

Unlocking mobility research while protecting the people behind the data.

Human mobility data can reshape transportation, public health, urban planning, and network design. Yet the most valuable data is often proprietary, expensive, uneven, or too sensitive to share.

MobilityNet creates a shared, open-source cyberinfrastructure that transforms cross-domain mobility signals into useful, interpretable, and privacy-preserving synthetic datasets. The platform supports rigorous research without requiring direct access to sensitive individual traces.

Supported by the U.S. National Science FoundationAwards #2411151 · #2411152 · #2411153

02 / Research

From fragmented signals
to trusted synthetic data.

Three connected research layers form the core of MobilityNet.

01

Cross-domain data curation

Integrate cellular, transportation, payment-network, and urban-sensor signals into a unified corpus, addressing the bias and fragmentation of single-source mobility datasets.

CONNECT
02

Socially informed interpretation

Use large language models to enrich sparse mobility records with context about activity purpose, behavioral patterns, and urban dynamics, making raw signals more scientifically meaningful.

UNDERSTAND
03

Privacy-preserving generation

Develop diffusion-based generative models with privacy and fairness constraints, balancing fidelity, analytical utility, demographic representation, and protection from re-identification.
